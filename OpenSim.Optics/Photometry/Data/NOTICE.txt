CIE data tables shipped with OpenSim Studio (plan item OP-79)

CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv
  "Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer", International
  Commission on Illumination (CIE), Vienna, 2019. DOI 10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b.
  Original source: CIE 018:2019, Table 6. 360-830 nm in 1 nm steps.
  https://files.cie.co.at/Publications-datasets/CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv

CIE_sle_photopic.csv
  "CIE spectral luminous efficiency for photopic vision", International Commission on
  Illumination (CIE), Vienna, 2019. Original source: CIE 018:2019, Table 1. 360-830 nm in 1 nm
  steps, wavelengths in standard air.
  https://files.cie.co.at/Publications-datasets/CIE_sle_photopic.csv
